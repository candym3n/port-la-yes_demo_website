About Us

Brand Story

Port La Yes is an upcoming small theoretical brand business in South Africa, Sandton. This is a beauty and skincare business that offers affordable/ high-quality products. It is not for everyone, though. The focus is to help young women express themselves. The products range from lipstick to face stickers.The business was created to make everyday beauty products accessible to everyone while promoting self-care and self-expression.

Mission Statement

Our mission is to empower individuals to express their unique beauty and feel confident in their own skin. We are committed to providing high-quality, affordable beauty and skincare products that cater to diverse needs and preferences.

Vision Statement

Our vision is to become a leading beauty and skincare brand that inspires self-expression and promotes confidence in all individuals.

How the Business Started?

Port La Yes was founded by a beauty enthusiasts who wanted to create a brand that celebrates individuality and empowers people to express themselves through makeup and skincare. The founder recognized the need for affordable, high-quality products that cater to diverse skin tones and types, and they set out to create a brand that would fill that gap in the market. Starting as a small bussiness, the brand aims to building a community of beauty lovers who share the same values and passion for self-expression.
